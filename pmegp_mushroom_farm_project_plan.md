# PROJECT MANAGEMENT PLAN: PMEGP LOAN DISBURSAL & MUSHROOM FARM ESTABLISHMENT

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
This comprehensive project plan outlines the end-to-end process for securing a loan under the **Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP)** and establishing a commercial mushroom cultivation farm from scratch. The project encompasses legal structuring, application preparation, bank liaison, infrastructure setup, training, and commercial launch.

---

## 2. WORK BREAKDOWN STRUCTURE (WBS) & DEPLOYMENT SCHEDULE

### PHASE 1: PRE-APPLICATION & FEASIBILITY ANALYSIS
**Objective:** Complete preliminary research, secure training credentials, and prepare the core project documentation.

*   **Task 1.1: Entrepreneurship Development Programme (EDP) / Mushroom Cultivation Training**
    *   **Duration:** 10 Days
    *   **Start Date:** Day 1
    *   **End Date:** Day 10
    *   **Responsible Party:** Promoter / Entrepreneur & KVIC-approved training institute
    *   **Deliverables/Outcomes:** Valid Mushroom Cultivation Training Certificate and EDP completion certificate (mandatory for PMEGP).
*   **Task 1.2: Detailed Project Report (DPR) Formulation**
    *   **Duration:** 7 Days
    *   **Start Date:** Day 11
    *   **End Date:** Day 17
    *   **Responsible Party:** Financial Consultant / Promoter
    *   **Deliverables/Outcomes:** Finalized DPR specifying capital expenditure (CapEx) for cropping rooms, recurring working capital (OpEx), projected cash flows, and IRR.
*   **Task 1.3: Statutory Registrations & Document Gathering**
    *   **Duration:** 5 Days
    *   **Start Date:** Day 18
    *   **End Date:** Day 22
    *   **Responsible Party:** Promoter
    *   **Deliverables/Outcomes:** Aadhaar card, PAN card, Caste/Special Category Certificate (if applicable), Land/Lease agreement papers, and Udyam Registration.

### PHASE 2: PMEGP PORTAL APPLICATION & IN-PRINCIPLE APPROVAL
**Objective:** Digital submission, agency vetting, and securing bank sanction.

*   **Task 2.1: Online Application Submission via Kviconline Portal**
    *   **Duration:** 2 Days
    *   **Start Date:** Day 23
    *   **End Date:** Day 24
    *   **Responsible Party:** Promoter
    *   **Deliverables/Outcomes:** Successfully generated PMEGP Application ID; documents routed to the selected Nodal Agency (KVIC/KVIB/DIC).
*   **Task 2.2: Agency Vetting and Forwarding to Bank**
    *   **Duration:** 20 Days
    *   **Start Date:** Day 25
    *   **End Date:** Day 44
    *   **Responsible Party:** District Task Force Committee (DTFC) / Nodal Agency
    *   **Deliverables/Outcomes:** Interview clearance and official forwarding of the application to the preferred Financing Bank Branch.
*   **Task 2.3: Bank Credit Appraisal & Sanction Letter Issuance**
    *   **Duration:** 25 Days
    *   **Start Date:** Day 45
    *   **End Date:** Day 69
    *   **Responsible Party:** Bank Branch Manager / Credit Officer
    *   **Deliverables/Outcomes:** Formal Bank Sanction Letter for Term Loan and Working Capital, detailing interest rates and repayment terms.

### PHASE 3: FUND DISBURSAL & SUBSIDY CLAIM LOCK-IN
**Objective:** Equity contribution payment, first loan tranche release, and margin money reservation.

*   **Task 3.1: Own Contribution Deposit & Loan Agreement Signing**
    *   **Duration:** 4 Days
    *   **Start Date:** Day 70
    *   **End Date:** Day 73
    *   **Responsible Party:** Promoter & Bank Operations Team
    *   **Deliverables/Outcomes:** Deposit receipt of 5% to 10% own equity; executed loan agreements and hypothecation deeds.
*   **Task 3.2: First Tranche Disbursal & Margin Money (Subsidy) Online Claim**
    *   **Duration:** 5 Days
    *   **Start Date:** Day 74
    *   **End Date:** Day 78
    *   **Responsible Party:** Financing Bank Branch
    *   **Deliverables/Outcomes:** Disbursement of funds into the project account; online submission of Margin Money claim to KVIC portal. Lock-in of subsidy in a 3-year Term Deposit Receipt (TDR).

### PHASE 4: CIVIL STRUCTURE & COMPONENT SOURCING
**Objective:** Building the physical cultivation environment and procurement of machinery.

*   **Task 4.1: Site Preparation & Cultivation Room Construction**
    *   **Duration:** 30 Days
    *   **Start Date:** Day 79
    *   **End Date:** Day 108
    *   **Responsible Party:** Civil Contractor / Promoter
    *   **Deliverables/Outcomes:** Insulated cropping rooms (Button/Oyster specific layout) complete with standard flooring and drainage infrastructure.
*   **Task 4.2: HVAC and Climate Control System Installation**
    *   **Duration:** 12 Days
    *   **Start Date:** Day 109
    *   **End Date:** Day 120
    *   **Responsible Party:** HVAC Vendor
    *   **Deliverables/Outcomes:** Installation of Air Handling Units (AHUs), humidifiers, temperature sensors, and ventilation systems ensuring 15-18°C capability.
*   **Task 4.3: Racks Setup and Composting/Spawning Equipment Procurement**
    *   **Duration:** 8 Days
    *   **Start Date:** Day 121
    *   **End Date:** Day 128
    *   **Responsible Party:** Fabrication Vendor
    *   **Deliverables/Outcomes:** Multi-tier vertical iron/GI pipe racks installed maximizing spatial footprint.

### PHASE 5: INPUT PROCUREMENT, INOCULATION & CYCLING
**Objective:** Substrate preparation, spawning, and cropping initialization.

*   **Task 5.1: Raw Material Sourcing (Substrate & Spawn)**
    *   **Duration:** 5 Days
    *   **Start Date:** Day 129
    *   **End Date:** Day 133
    *   **Responsible Party:** Procurement In-charge / Promoter
    *   **Deliverables/Outcomes:** Bulk arrival of wheat straw/paddy straw, supplements (gypsum, bran), and high-quality lab-certified mushroom spawn (mycelium).
*   **Task 5.2: Composting, Pasteurization, and Bag Filling**
    *   **Duration:** 15 Days
    *   **Start Date:** Day 134
    *   **End Date:** Day 148
    *   **Responsible Party:** Farm Labor / Technical Supervisor
    *   **Deliverables/Outcomes:** Properly pasteurized substrate bags spawned and transferred into dark incubation rooms for spawn run.
*   **Task 5.3: Casing Application & Pinhead Induction (Spawn Run)**
    *   **Duration:** 18 Days
    *   **Start Date:** Day 149
    *   **End Date:** Day 166
    *   **Responsible Party:** Technical Supervisor
    *   **Deliverables/Outcomes:** Mycelium fully colonizes beds; application of treated casing soil layer; environment altered to trigger pinhead formation.

### PHASE 6: HARVESTING, POST-HARVEST MANAGEMENT, & SUBSIDY VERIFICATION
**Objective:** Commercial yield extraction, sales execution, and physical verification for subsidy release.

*   **Task 6.1: Harvesting and Packaging**
    *   **Duration:** 7 Days
    *   **Start Date:** Day 167
    *   **End Date:** Day 173
    *   **Responsible Party:** Farm Labor / Packing Team
    *   **Deliverables/Outcomes:** Premium grade fresh mushrooms harvested daily, sorted, vacuum packed, and kept in cold storage.
*   **Task 6.2: Supply Chain Logistics & Commercial Sales Launch**
    *   **Duration:** Ongoing (Starts Day 170)
    *   **Start Date:** Day 170
    *   **End Date:** Day 175
    *   **Responsible Party:** Marketing Lead / Promoter
    *   **Deliverables/Outcomes:** Daily dispatch to local wholesale mandis, retail supermarket chains, and restaurants. First sales revenue realization.
*   **Task 6.3: Joint Physical Verification for Subsidy Release**
    *   **Duration:** 1 Day (scheduled within window)
    *   **Start Date:** Day 180
    *   **End Date:** Day 180
    *   **Responsible Party:** KVIC/DIC Officials, Bank Manager, and Promoter
    *   **Deliverables/Outcomes:** Successful physical verification report filed confirming active project run. Approval for the ultimate release of Margin Money credit to the loan account post lock-in.
