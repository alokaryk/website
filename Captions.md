<div data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep"><span data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Dynamic word-by-word kinetic captions<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span> (popularized by creators like Alex Hormozi and MrBeast) drastically increase video engagement. You can easily create these on a Windows laptop using either <mark data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep"><span data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Submagic<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span> (a web-based AI power-tool) or <span data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">AutoCap<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span> (an app that can be emulated or used via its web framework)<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></mark>.<span data-sfc-root="ep"> [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6] <!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span><!--TgQPHd|||[]--></div>

<div data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Submagic is completely cloud-based, meaning it runs directly in your Windows browser (Chrome, Edge, etc.) without needing a heavy installation.<span data-sfc-root="ep"> [4] <!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span><!--TgQPHd|||[]--></div>
<h2 data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Step 1: Upload and Transcribe<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></h2>
<div data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">
<div>&nbsp;</div>
<ol>
<li data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Open your web browser and go to the official Submagic Website. Log in or create a free trial account.<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></li>
<li data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Click <span data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Generate Captions<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span> from your dashboard.<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></li>
<li data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep"><span data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Drag and drop<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span> your exported MP4 video into the upload area (or click to browse your files).<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></li>
<li data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Select the <span data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">spoken language<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span> of your video (e.g., English, Spanish, Hinglish) and click <span data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Upload<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span>. The AI will automatically transcribe your video word-by-word in seconds.<span data-sfc-root="ep"> [4, 7, 8, 9] <!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span><!--TgQPHd|||[]--></li>
</ol>

<div data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">
<div>&nbsp;</div>
<ol>
<li data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Once the video loads, click on the <span data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Style<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span> tab in the left/top sidebar.<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></li>
<li data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">You will see preset kinetic themes named after top creators (e.g., <span data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Hormozi<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span>, <span data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Mr Beast<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span>, <span data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Leon<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span>, or <span data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Dev<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span>). Click on a theme to instantly apply it.<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></li>
<li data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Click the <span data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Play<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span> button on the video preview to see the kinetic motion. The text will dynamically light up word-by-word as you speak.<span data-sfc-root="ep"> [1, 2, 10, 11] <!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span><!--TgQPHd|||[]--></li>
</ol>

<h2 data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Step 3: Fine-Tune and Customise<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></h2>
<div data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">
<div>&nbsp;</div>
<ol>
<li data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Click <span data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Edit Theme<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span> under the Style menu to adjust the look. You can change the font type, size, and secondary text colors.<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></li>
<li data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Switch to the <span data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Captions<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span> tab. Here, you will see a text transcript broke down word-by-word.<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></li>
<li data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">If the AI misspelled a word, click directly on it to correct the spelling. You can also click the emoji icon next to any word to add or change the animated emojis that flash with the text.<span data-sfc-root="ep"> [1, 2, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12] <!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span><!--TgQPHd|||[]--></li>
</ol>

<div>&nbsp;</div>
<ol>
<li data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Once you are satisfied with the animations and text accuracy, click the <span data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Export<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span> button in the top right corner.<!--TgQPHd|||[]--></li>
<li data-sfc-cp="" data-sfc-root="ep">Submagic will render the video with "burned-in" kinetic captions. Download the finished MP4 file directly onto your Windows laptop.<span data-sfc-root="ep"> [4, 11, 13] <!--TgQPHd|||[]--></span><!--TgQPHd|||[]--></li>
</ol>
